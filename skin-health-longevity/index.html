    How will my skin age?

    Everyone's skin ages differently, but the common changes are fine lines, loss of firmness, uneven pigmentation, larger pores, dryness and a rougher texture. How fast they appear depends on your genes, sun exposure, smoking and how you look after your skin.

    What ages skin the most?

    Sunlight. Unprotected sun exposure is behind most visible ageing, which is why daily SPF comes first in any plan. Smoking is the next largest factor you can control.

    What are the five habits?

    Daily SPF, clinical-grade home skincare, skin boosters, collagen stimulators such as micro-needling, and regular chemical exfoliation.

    Is this a prediction?

    No. It is an illustration to start a conversation, not a diagnosis or a forecast for you personally. A consultation gives you a plan based on your own skin.
