      How much does laser hair removal cost?

      It depends on the area. At Skintology small areas such as the upper lip or underarms start from £20 to £25 a session, and larger areas such as full legs cost more. The calculator adds up your exact areas.

      How many laser sessions will I need?

      Most people see their best result after 6 to 8 sessions, spaced 4 to 8 weeks apart, because hair grows in cycles and laser treats the hairs that are actively growing.

      Is it cheaper to treat areas together?

      Often, yes. Combined areas such as upper lip and chin, or full legs with bikini, are priced lower than the same areas booked separately. The calculator points this out.

      Is laser hair removal permanent?

      Laser gives long-term hair reduction. Most people keep a lasting reduction after a full course, and some choose an occasional top-up session.
