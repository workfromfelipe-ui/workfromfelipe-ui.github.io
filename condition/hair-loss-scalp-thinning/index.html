Hair Loss & Scalp Thinning

Regenerative Solutions for Follicle Health and Hair Density.

Hair loss is a complex condition often linked to genetics, hormonal shifts, or a decline in the scalp’s structural integrity. Our medical-grade approach focuses on revitalising dormant follicles and improving the scalp environment from the lower levels upward to encourage thicker, healthier hair growth.

Understanding Hair Loss

Hair loss occurs when the natural growth cycle is disrupted, or when hair follicles begin to shrink (miniaturise). This can be caused by a lack of essential nutrients reaching the scalp, decreased blood flow, or cellular slowdown. Our hair thinning treatment combines microneedling, a specialist scalp solution and LED light therapy to support the scalp and target follicle shrinkage, helping to improve hair density and strength.

Symptoms

  • General Thinning

    A noticeable reduction in hair volume across the top or crown of the head.

  • Receding Hairline

    The gradual shifting of the hairline back from the forehead, a common sign of androgenetic alopecia.

  • Widening Part

    The line of the hair part becoming more prominent as individual hair strands become finer.

  • Excessive Shedding

    Noticing significantly more hair than usual in the brush, shower, or on pillows.

  • Lacklustre Quality

    Hair that appears brittle, dry, or lacks the vibrant "snap-back" of healthy strands.

Questions people ask

Is the treatment suitable for all types of hair loss?

Our hair thinning treatment is most effective for thinning hair and early-stage hair loss where follicles are still active. We provide a full assessment to determine if this is the right path for you.

How soon will I see results?

While some patients notice a reduction in shedding early on, the full results of hair regrowth and increased density typically take 3 to 6 months to become visible as new hair grows.

How many sessions are recommended?

For optimal results, we generally recommend a course of 3 to 6 sessions spaced 4 to 6 weeks apart, followed by maintenance treatments every 6 to 12 months.
