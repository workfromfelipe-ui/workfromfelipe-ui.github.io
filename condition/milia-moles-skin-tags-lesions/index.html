Understanding Skin Lesions

Skin lesions are areas of the skin that have an abnormal growth or appearance compared to the surrounding tissue. While most are benign (non-cancerous), they can affect your confidence or become irritated by clothing and jewelry. These imperfections can occur due to genetics, the natural ageing process, or environmental factors such as UV exposure.

Symptoms

  • Milia

    Small, firm white bumps often found around the eyes or cheeks, caused by trapped keratin beneath the skin's surface.

  • Skin Tags

    Soft, flesh-colored growths that hang off the skin, commonly appearing in areas where skin rubs together, such as the neck or underarms.

  • Moles

    Pigmented spots that can be flat or raised; while usually harmless, any changes in shape or color should be professionally assessed.

  • Cherry Angiomas

    Small, bright red circular spots caused by an overgrowth of blood vessels near the surface.

  • Warts & Verrucas

    Rough, grainy growths caused by viral infections that can persist without clinical intervention.

Questions people ask

Is lesion removal painful?

Most removal methods, such as Cryotherapy or Electrolysis, involve only a brief stinging or "biting" sensation that subsides almost immediately.

Will the removal leave a scar?

Our precision techniques are designed to minimize the risk of scarring. Most lesions heal completely, leaving the skin smooth and clear, though individual healing times vary.

Do I need a doctor's note for mole removal?

At Skintology, safety is our priority. We require a professional assessment of all moles prior to treatment, and in some cases, we may request a GP's confirmation that the mole is benign before proceeding with aesthetic removal.
