Body Pigmentation & Sun Damage

Correcting Discolouration and Restoring Uniform Body Tone.

Body pigmentation often results from cumulative UV exposure, hormonal shifts, or post-inflammatory responses to injury or acne. Our clinical approach focuses on breaking down excess melanin and regulating skin behavior from the lower levels upward to restore a clear, even-toned appearance.

Understanding Body Pigmentation

The skin on the body, though thicker than facial skin, is equally susceptible to pigmentary changes. When melanocytes (pigment-producing cells) become overactive or damaged, they produce an uneven distribution of melanin. This can manifest as isolated spots or larger patches of discolouration. Because body skin has a different cellular turnover rate, it requires medical-grade technologies to penetrate effectively and stimulate structural repair.

Symptoms

  • Solar Lentigines (Sun Spots)

    Flat, brown spots typically found on high-exposure areas like the shoulders, chest, and hands.

  • Poikiloderma of Civatte

    A combination of pigmentation, redness, and thinning skin commonly seen on the sides of the neck and chest.

  • Post-Inflammatory Hyperpigmentation (PIH)

    Darkened marks left behind after body acne, scratches, or surgical scars have healed.

  • Mottled Skin Tone

    A general "patchy" or uneven appearance across larger surface areas like the legs or back.

  • Friction-Induced Darkening

    Darkening in areas where skin frequently rubs together or against tight clothing.

Questions people ask

Is treatment for body pigmentation painful?

Most patients describe the sensation of laser treatments as a mild "flick" or tingling. Our Cutera systems feature integrated cooling to ensure your comfort throughout the session.

How many sessions will I need?

While superficial sun damage may clear in 1 to 3 sessions, deeper pigmentation or larger areas typically require a course of 4 to 6 sessions for optimal clarity.

Can I treat pigmentation while I have a tan?

We advise waiting until your natural tan has faded before beginning laser treatments to ensure the technology can safely distinguish between the target pigment and your natural skin tone.
