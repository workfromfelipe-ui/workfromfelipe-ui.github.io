Award-Winning Skin Tightening, Lifting, and Resurfacing.

We are proud to offer Endymed 3DEEP®, the gold standard in non-invasive radiofrequency technology. What is Endymed 3DEEP®?

Endymed 3DEEP® is a patented, multi-source radiofrequency technology that delivers deep, volumetric heating into the skin’s foundation. By utilizing six independent RF generators, it pushes energy significantly deeper than standard “bipolar” treatments, all while keeping the surface of your skin cool and comfortable. This “controlled trauma” triggers a powerful natural healing response, restoring structural integrity and firming the complexion without surgery.

Which Endymed Treatment is Right for Me?

  • 3DEEP Tightening

    This "surface glide" mode uses deep, soothing heat to lift and firm sagging skin. It is the perfect choice for treating jowls and the neck, or for achieving an immediate "red-carpet" glow.

  • RF Needling (Intensif)

    By combining deep heat with medical-grade micro-needles, this mode targets the skin’s structure from the inside out. It is the gold standard for treating acne scars, deep wrinkles, and stretch marks.

  • FSR Resurfacing

    This fractional heat technology creates a "grid" of micro-resurfacing on the skin’s surface. It is best for refining large pores, smoothing rough texture, and repairing visible sun damage.

  • EndyBleph

    A targeted hybrid protocol specifically designed for the delicate eye area. It combines tightening and resurfacing to provide a non-surgical lift for hooded eyelids and "crepey" under-eye skin.

Questions people ask

Does it hurt?

The 3DEEP Tightening mode is often compared to a relaxing hot stone massage. RF Needling and FSR involve a different sensation, but we focus on your comfort and use advanced techniques to ensure a positive experience.

How soon will I see results?

Many patients notice an immediate "lifting" effect due to collagen fibers contracting. However, the most significant results appear over 3 to 6 months as your body produces a fresh supply of new collagen.

Is there any downtime?

Tightening treatments have zero downtime. Resurfacing and Needling may result in 24–48 hours of redness, similar to a mild sunburn, followed by a professional aftercare routine including face SPF.
