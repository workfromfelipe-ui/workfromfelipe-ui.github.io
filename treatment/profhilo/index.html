Regenerative Fat Restoration for a Lifted, Contoured Profile.

Profhilo® Structura is the latest advancement in our “active ageing” repertoire, a pioneering injectable treatment specifically designed to target and regenerate superficial facial fat, helping you restore your facial contours naturally from the lower levels upward.

Book now

What is Profhilo®?

While the original Profhilo® is renowned for its skin-remodeling and hydrating properties, Structura goes deeper. It is the first injectable treatment designed to address “lipo-lifting”—the restoration and repositioning of superficial fat layers that shift and flatten as we age. Using highly concentrated Hyaluronic Acid, it signals the body to repair and regenerate adipose (fat) tissue, providing a natural lift without the “puffy” or artificial look sometimes associated with traditional dermal fillers.

Classic Profhilo® uses a high concentration of pure hyaluronic acid that spreads evenly beneath the skin, deeply hydrating and remodelling it to improve firmness and elasticity across the face and neck. Many clients start with Classic for skin quality, then add Structura when they want lift as well.

Questions people ask

How does it differ from traditional dermal fillers?

Fillers work by "filling" space mechanically to add volume. Structura is a biostimulator; it doesn't just sit in the skin, it encourages your body to regenerate its own fat and collagen for a much more natural, "biological" lift.

What is the treatment process like?

Structura is typically administered using a blunt-tipped cannula via a single entry point in front of the ear. This advanced technique ensures the product reaches the correct fat compartment while significantly reducing the risk of bruising.

How many sessions are required?

For optimal results, we recommend a course of two sessions spaced 4 weeks apart. Results are gradual, with the most significant lifting and firming appearing about a month after your second treatment.

Should I choose Classic Profhilo® or Structura?

Classic focuses on hydration and skin quality across the face and neck. Structura works on the deeper fat layers at the side of the face to restore lift and contour. Your practitioner will recommend one, or both, at your consultation.
