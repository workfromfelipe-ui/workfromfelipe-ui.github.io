Why Choose Clinical Chemical Peels?

Chemical peels are a powerful tool for active ageing and skin correction. By applying a controlled, medical-grade solution, we remove the outer layers of dead skin cells to address a range of conditions.

Key Benefits of Chemical Peels

  • Acne & Blemish Control

    Deeply cleanses pores and reduces excess sebum production to prevent future breakouts.

  • Hyperpigmentation & Melasma

    Fades dark patches, age spots, and post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation (PIH) for an even skin tone.

  • Fine Lines & Wrinkles

    Stimulates the production of collagen and elastin, leaving the skin looking plumper and more youthful.

  • Textural Irregularities

    Smooths rough patches, treats "bumpy skin" (Keratosis Pilaris), and minimizes the appearance of enlarged pores.

Questions people ask

What exactly is a chemical skin peel?

It is a professional exfoliation treatment that uses Alpha Hydroxy Acids (AHAs) or Beta Hydroxy Acids (BHAs) to remove dead skin cells from the surface, revealing the brighter, fresher skin underneath.

How do peels help with ageing?

Peels help reduce the appearance of fine lines and wrinkles while stimulating the production of collagen and elastin for a plumper, more youthful complexion.

Will a chemical peel help my acne?

Yes, specific acids like Salicylic Acid are oil-soluble and penetrate deep into the pores to decrease oil production, unclog congestion, and minimize bacterial growth.

Can I have a peel if I have sensitive skin?

Absolutely. While stronger acids like Glycolic might be too intense, we use gentler alternatives like Lactic Acid or Polyhydroxy Acids (PHAs) that exfoliate without causing irritation.

Is there anything I should do before my treatment?

We recommend a consultation to assess your skin type. It is also vital to ensure your skin is healthy and not currently sunburnt.

What should I expect after the treatment?

Depending on the strength of the peel, you may experience mild redness or actual "peeling" as the dead skin cells shed to reveal a smoother texture.

Do I need to wear sunscreen after a peel?

Yes, this is mandatory. Chemical exfoliation makes your skin significantly more sensitive to sunlight, so you must apply a face SPF every single day to prevent UV damage and pigmentation.

How many treatments will I need to see results?

While you may notice an instant "glow," we generally recommend consistent treatments every 2 to 3 weeks for the best long-term results in addressing texture and dark spots.
