What is Tear Trough Filler?

The tear trough is the area between your lower eyelid and your upper cheek. As we get older, collagen and hyaluronic acid begin to degenerate, which can lead to a sunken appearance or dark shadows in this region. We use a specialized Hyaluronic Acid filler, a hydrator naturally found in your skin, to delicately fill these hollows, drawing moisture to the area and plumping the complexion from within.

Alongside tear trough filler, we offer non-surgical treatments for every concern around the eyes. EndyMed Pro RF Eyes uses gentle radiofrequency to tighten the delicate skin and soften fine lines. EndyBleph combines radiofrequency with fractional skin resurfacing (FSR) to improve firmness and texture on the upper and lower eyelids, softening crepey skin and hooded eyes. For hydration and skin quality, Polynucleotides and Sunekos® skin boosters can be used beneath the eyes to refresh tired-looking skin.

Questions people ask

Is the procedure safe for the eye area?

Yes. We use medical-grade fillers and advanced techniques tailored specifically for the thin, delicate skin of the tear trough.

How long does the treatment take?

The procedure is relatively quick, usually taking around 35 to 40 minutes, making it a convenient option for those with busy schedules.

Will I see results immediately?

Yes, the volumising effect of the Hyaluronic Acid is visible straight away, though final results will settle once any minor swelling subsides.

Which eye treatment is right for me?

It depends on your main concern: hollowness, loose or crepey skin, or fine lines. Your practitioner will assess the area at your consultation and recommend a single treatment or a combination.
