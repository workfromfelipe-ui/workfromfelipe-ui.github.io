    How this works

    A short version of the Fitzpatrick skin type scale, the one practitioners use to choose safe laser settings, with the extra questions we ask before any laser treatment.

    1. Answer seven questions about your natural skin tone, eye colour, how you burn and tan, and the hair you want treated.
    2. Each answer is scored, and the total places you on the Fitzpatrick scale from Type I to Type VI.
    3. You see your likely type, whether laser hair removal is likely to suit you, and what to raise at your consultation.
    What is my Fitzpatrick skin type?

    The Fitzpatrick scale groups skin into six types by how it reacts to sun. Type I always burns and never tans; Type VI is deeply pigmented and does not burn. This checker estimates yours from your skin tone, eye colour and how you burn and tan.

    Does laser hair removal work on dark skin?

    Yes, with the right laser and settings. Deeper skin tones need a system and a practitioner experienced with them, which is why we check your skin type before treating.

    Does laser work on blonde, red or grey hair?

    Laser targets the pigment in the hair, so it works best on dark hair. Blonde, red, grey and white hair has little pigment and usually responds poorly.

    Can I have laser with a tan?

    A recent tan or self-tan changes how skin absorbs laser light and raises the risk of a reaction. You will usually be asked to wait until it has faded.

    Is this a diagnosis?

    No. It is guidance to help you prepare. Your practitioner confirms your skin type and suitability at a consultation, with a patch test where needed.
