How this works

  1. Pick one area or several, and say whether you wax or shave now.

Is laser hair removal cheaper than waxing?

Over time, usually yes. Waxing is a cost that never ends, while laser is mostly paid for in the first year. For regular waxers laser typically pays for itself within one to three years, depending on the area.

Is laser cheaper than shaving?

Not always on money alone, because razors are cheap. The saving with shaving is mostly time, along with fewer ingrown hairs and less irritation. The calculator shows both honestly.

How is the laser cost worked out?

It uses Skintology's price per session for your area, a course of 8 sessions, then one top-up session a year. Your own plan is confirmed at a consultation and may need fewer sessions.

How much does the average person spend on waxing?

It varies widely by area and salon. That is why the calculator starts with a typical price and lets you change it to exactly what you pay.
